---
title: Research
subtitle: Expanding women's health research across the lifespan.
---

WHIRC research spans public health, psychology, bioengineering, and artificial
intelligence. Our work is organized around conditions that uniquely or
disproportionately affect women, and the data and methods needed to study them.

WHIRC is funded by a University of Maryland
[Grand Challenges Institutional Grant](https://research.umd.edu/grand-challenges-grants/grand-challenges-2/institutional-grants-2.0/whirc),
which supports interdisciplinary research, seed funding for new projects, and
training for the next generation of women's health researchers.
