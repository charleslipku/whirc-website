---
title: Affinity Research Teams
subtitle: Faculty-led teams organized around WHIRC's four core research areas.
---

WHIRC organizes its research through **Affinity Research Teams**: groups of
affiliated faculty, staff, and trainees who share an interest in one of our four
core research areas. Faculty opt in to one or more teams, and each team shapes
its own research agenda, collaborations, and funding proposals.

## Maternal & Reproductive Health

Maternal mortality and morbidity, fertility, pregnancy outcomes, and
reproductive health across the lifespan.

## Gendered Stress & Mental Health

The health impact of gendered racism and intersectional stressors on women's
wellbeing.

## Cardiovascular & Cellular Health

Sex differences in vascular biology and cardiovascular disease mechanisms.

## AI for Women's Health

Addressing bias in health AI caused by the underrepresentation of women in
training data, and building equitable machine-learning tools for women's health.

*Team descriptions and faculty leads will be expanded as members join each
team. Interested in joining a team? See how to [get involved](/get-involved/#join-whirc).*
