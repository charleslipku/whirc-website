## Women's Health Interdisciplinary Research Collaborative

**University of Maryland Grand Challenges Institutional Grant**

WHIRC's founding award unites faculty from public health, psychology,
bioengineering, and artificial intelligence to advance translational research,
develop a diverse workforce pipeline, and build community partnerships that
improve women's health across the lifespan.
[View the official project page →](https://research.umd.edu/grand-challenges-grants/grand-challenges-2/institutional-grants-2.0/whirc)
