Featuring **Margaret Slavin, PhD, RDN** (Associate Professor of Nutrition and
Food Science) and **Brantley Hall, PhD** (Associate Professor of Biological
Sciences).

The 2027 Women's Health Interdisciplinary Research Spring Seminar Series brings
together faculty from the University of Maryland, College Park and the
University of Maryland, Baltimore to explore health conditions that affect women
and people assigned female at birth *disproportionately, exclusively, or
differently*, and why accounting for sex-based differences leads to better
science and better care for everyone. Meeting information is sent to the email
used at registration.
