---
title: "Spring Seminar Series: Nutrition and Migraines Among Women"
date: 2027-02-09
---

Featuring **Margaret Slavin** and **Brantley Hall**.

The 2027 Women's Health Interdisciplinary Research Spring Seminar Series brings
together faculty from the University of Maryland, College Park and the
University of Maryland, Baltimore to explore health conditions that affect women
and people assigned female at birth *disproportionately, exclusively, or
differently*, with talks on nutrition and migraine, reproductive health policy,
and cardiometabolic health and hormones.
